Privacy Policy

Your information, plainly.

Effective June 11, 2026 · Finish Line Home Repair, Lafayette, Indiana

Finish Line Home Repair ("we," "us") is an owner-operated home repair business serving Greater Lafayette, Indiana. This policy explains what information this website collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect only what you choose to send us when requesting a quote, we use it to respond and do the work, and we never sell it.

Information you give us

When you submit our quote form or contact us, we receive what you provide:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • The description of your repair project, which may include a property address
  • Any file you choose to attach, such as a home inspection report

Inspection reports and project details often contain information about a property and transaction. We treat them as confidential, use them only to prepare your quote and perform the work, and share them with no one else.

Information collected automatically

Like nearly every website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and to operate the site. We don't use this information to identify or profile visitors. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests those font files from Google's servers when the page loads.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and we do not currently use any analytics service. If we add a basic analytics tool in the future (such as Google Analytics) to understand site traffic, we will update this policy to say so before or when it happens.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and communicate about scheduling and work
  • To prepare estimates and keep ordinary business records of jobs
  • To meet legal, tax, and insurance obligations that apply to any business

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who handles your data for us

We use a small number of service providers to run this site, and they process data only to provide their service to us:

  • FormSubmit, which delivers quote-form submissions (including attachments) to our email
  • Cloudflare, which hosts and secures this website
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces used on the site
  • Our email provider, where your messages and form submissions are received and stored

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and job records as long as reasonably needed to serve you and to meet ordinary business, warranty, tax, and insurance requirements. After that, we delete them. If you'd like something deleted sooner, just ask.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we have about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it. You can also simply call (765) 202-3621 instead of using the form, and nothing is collected through the website at all.

Children

This site is for adults arranging home repair services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and form submissions are transmitted encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we limit what we collect to what's needed to quote and complete your project.

Changes to this policy

If we change how this site handles information, for example by adding analytics, we'll update this page and its effective date.
