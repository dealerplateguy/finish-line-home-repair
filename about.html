Our Story

Quality Repairs, Responsive Service

Finish Line Home Repair was founded to provide professional, dependable home repair services with a strong focus on real estate transactions and inspection repair punch lists.

With over 20 years of maintenance and repair experience, we understand how important communication, reliability, and timing are for homeowners and real estate professionals alike.

Our goal is simple: provide quality repairs, responsive service, and a smooth experience from start to finish. We proudly serve Lafayette, West Lafayette, and surrounding Indiana communities.
