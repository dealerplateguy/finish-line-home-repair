Professional Home Repair Services

From a single inspection item to a complete punch list, we handle the common repairs that keep homes safe, functional, and move-in ready across Greater Lafayette.

01

Inspection Repair Punch Lists

Fast completion of inspection-related repair items to help buyers, sellers, and realtors keep closings on schedule. Hand us the report and we'll quote and complete the list as one coordinated project — no juggling multiple trades.

02

Drywall Repair & Paint Touch-Ups

Wall patching, nail pops, stress cracks, texture repair, caulking, and paint touch-ups that leave walls clean and ready. Ideal for pre-listing prep and inspection corrections in Lafayette and West Lafayette homes.

03

Minor Electrical Repairs

Outlet and switch replacement, light fixtures, GFCI replacement, smoke detectors, and other common non-permitted repairs handled safely and to code-conscious standards.

04

Minor Plumbing Repairs

Faucets, drains, shutoff valves, toilets, garbage disposals, and minor fixture repairs — the small plumbing items that frequently appear on inspection reports.

05

Trim & Finish Carpentry

Baseboards, casing, trim, door adjustments, hardware, and cosmetic finish work that puts a clean, professional final touch on every room.

06

Doors & Hardware

Sticking or misaligned doors, latches and locks, handles, hinges, and weatherstripping adjusted or replaced so everything opens, closes, and seals the way it should.

07

Window Repairs

Window hardware, screens, seals, locks, and operation fixes to restore smooth function and address common inspection callouts.

08

Deck & Exterior Repairs

Deck repairs, downspouts, flashing, caulking, and other exterior maintenance items that protect the home and improve curb appeal before a sale.

09

Gutters & Downspouts

Cleaning, resecuring, and minor repairs to gutters and downspouts to keep water moving away from the foundation and off the inspection list.

10

General Handyman Services

A dependable single point of contact for the wide range of small repairs and fixes a home needs — if it's on your list, ask us about it.
